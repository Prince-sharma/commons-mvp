Commons · MVP

Spread what your best AI users do to everyone else, and prove it stuck.

Companies buy hundreds of Claude seats, and a small group of power users gets most of the value. Commons finds the work those people already do with Claude, turns it into skills colleagues can run, and shows which ones people are still using a month later.

How the MVP works

The prototype uses demo data. Use Viewing as in the top bar to switch between the admin and employee views.

1 · One required pluginThe company installs one Claude plugin. Its hooks report prompts, skill runs and files from Cowork and Claude Code. Claude's own telemetry export adds who each session belongs to.
2 · AnalyticsWho is active, how deeply people use Claude, what kinds of work they do, and which skills get used.
3 · SkillsThe company skill library, ranked by real runs. A queue turns repeated work that has no skill yet into drafted skills for an admin to publish.

Where it goes next

  1. Stage 0MVPSee how Claude is used and which skills exist.
  2. Weeks 1–6Prove it stuckWhich skills colleagues kept using after 30 days, and which big jobs have no skill.
  3. Weeks 7–12Put it in the flowOne-line tips inside Claude ("people on your team use /x for this"), with a holdback group to prove they caused the lift.
  4. Weeks 13–20Capture without effortTurn a session into a skill with one command, tested on real prompts. Retire skills nobody uses.
  5. Weeks 21–32Shared, forkable runsRun cards that colleagues can fork into their own Claude, with credit going to the person who shared.
  6. LaterBeyond one vendorThe same loop for ChatGPT, Copilot and Gemini.
